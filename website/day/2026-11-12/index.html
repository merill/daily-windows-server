Day in brief

AD FS FAQ ties unknown domain identifiers to controller discovery and CLDAP safeguards

An updated AD FS troubleshooting FAQ explains that unrecognized domain identifiers in username and password requests can trigger domain controller discovery, including DNS SRV queries and CLDAP pings. It recommends Extranet Smart Lockout on Windows Server 2019 and later, along with restricting outbound CLDAP traffic.
